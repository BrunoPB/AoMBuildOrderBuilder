# Greeks: actions per major god

Source: the installed game, build 19.17020 (extracted `gameplay\*` and string tables), unless a row says otherwise. Paths are relative to `gameplay\`. The data is in `actions.json`. "BO" means the action matters for a build order (`buildOrder: true`).

Greeks have **no** promotion, empower, relocate or convert commands. The archaic techs (`ArchaicAgeZeus/Hades/Poseidon`, `ArchaicAgeGreek`) add just one command, `SetVillagerToGather` on the Temple, and no `ActionEnable` or `…To…` promotion techs. What is god-specific for Greeks is passives, heroes and a few researches.

## Open points

- **Hero queue:** the files only carry the `SplitQueue` flag plus the loading-screen tip. Whether the hero queue runs at full speed alongside a busy TC is not stated (assumed yes). The engine has no support for it yet.
- **Hippocampus first spawn:** does the 60 s `delay` also hold back the first one after the first Dock? Owner check.
- **Argive Patronage Maintain:** does it stop at the pop cap, and does it pause while the Fortress trains? Not in the files.
- **Vaults of Erebus:** 1.75 is assumed to be per second (engine convention). No owner measurement yet.
- **Free Scylla/Carcinos without a Dock:** presumably nothing spawns (already in PENDING.md).
