# Code of Conduct

> **Português:** seja respeitoso. Sem ofensas, assédio ou preconceito. Discorde das ideias, não das pessoas.

This is a community of Age of Mythology players. Everyone is welcome, whatever their skill level, language or background.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with new players and first-time contributors.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Back game claims with sources or tests.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind (race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, nationality, disability…).
- Spam, advertising, or sharing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, e-mail the maintainer at [brunopenabaeta@gmail.com](mailto:brunopenabaeta@gmail.com).
